Online test

ICIQ-SF questionnaire

Think about how you have been over the past 4 weeks and answer the questions. It takes about 2 minutes.

The result is indicative and is not a diagnosis. Your answers are processed only in your browser: they are not stored or sent anywhere.

1. How often do you leak urine?
2. How much urine do you usually leak (whether you wear protection or not)?
3. Overall, how much does leaking urine interfere with your everyday life? Selected: 0
0 — not at all10 — a great deal
4. When does urine leak? Tick all that apply to you. This question does not affect the score, but it helps the doctor identify the type of symptoms.

ICIQ-SF — International Consultation on Incontinence Questionnaire – Short Form (Avery K. et al., Neurourol Urodyn 2004). Severity bands after Klovning A. et al., Neurourol Urodyn 2009. The questionnaire is owned by ICIQ.

How to use the result

For patients

Complete the test at home, print it or save it as a PDF and show it to your doctor. The result is a starting point for a conversation, not a diagnosis.

For doctors

The total score helps gauge symptom severity and track change during treatment; answers to the last question suggest the leading type of incontinence.
