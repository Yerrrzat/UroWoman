ICIQ-SF test

An international questionnaire doctors use to assess how often urine leaks and how much it affects life. Think about how you've been over the past 4 weeks. It takes about 2 minutes.

The result is indicative and is not a diagnosis. Your answers are processed in your browser. Understanding your result

The score from 0 to 21 is the sum of your answers to the first three questions. The higher the score, the more symptoms affect your life:

  • 0 — no leakage;
  • 1–5 — slight;
  • 6–12 — moderate;
  • 13–18 — severe;
  • 19–21 — very severe.

Even with a slight score it's worth discussing symptoms with a doctor: at an early stage, simple measures help — exercises and changing habits. Print your result and take it to your appointment together with a bladder diary. After 3 months of treatment you can retake the test to see the change.

ICIQ-SF — International Consultation on Incontinence Questionnaire – Short Form (Avery K, et al., Neurourol Urodyn 2004). Severity bands after Klovning A, et al., Neurourol Urodyn 2009. The questionnaire is owned by ICIQ.
