For patients

How assessment works

The aim of assessment is to understand your symptoms, rule out conditions that need different treatment, and choose a suitable care plan.

  1. Talking to your doctor. Describe what happens, when it started and how it affects your sleep, work and activities. Mention pregnancies, births, operations and medicines.
  2. A 3-day diary. Record the time and volume of each void, what you drink, strong urges and leakage episodes. The diary helps reveal patterns.
  3. Examination. The doctor assesses your general health and, if needed, the pelvic floor muscles, and may perform a cough test.
  4. Urine test and other investigations. These are ordered when indicated, for example to rule out infection or check for residual urine.
  5. Further assessment. Urodynamic testing or a urogynaecology referral is needed only in specific situations, not for everyone.

How to prepare

Bring a list of your medicines, previous test results, your completed diary and your ICIQ-SF result. You can tell the doctor openly what worries you and what outcome matters to you, such as returning to sport or sleeping through the night.

Important

Do not sharply restrict fluids or stop prescribed medicines before your assessment without discussing it with your doctor.
