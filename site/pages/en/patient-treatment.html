For patients

Care and treatment options

You and your doctor choose a treatment plan together, taking into account the type and severity of symptoms, your overall health, pregnancy plans and personal preferences.

Where care usually starts

  • explaining the cause of symptoms and agreeing on goals;
  • regular pelvic floor muscle training;
  • bladder training for strong urges;
  • lifestyle changes: adequate fluid intake, treating constipation, gradual weight loss if needed.

When other methods are needed

For urgency symptoms, a doctor may suggest medicines. For significant stress incontinence, surgical options are discussed after assessment. Every method has expected benefits, risks and alternatives.

How to judge results

Do not expect change overnight. Your doctor will usually agree a follow-up date and assess not only how often leakage occurs but also your everyday comfort. Tell your doctor if treatment causes side effects or symptoms get worse.

Important

Do not start medicines or choose surgery on your own. Treatment is prescribed and monitored by a qualified specialist.
