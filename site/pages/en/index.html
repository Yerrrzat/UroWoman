You do not have to manage this alone

Urinary incontinence is common, and it is always appropriate to seek medical help.

What is urinary incontinence

Urinary incontinence is the involuntary leakage of urine. It affects women's quality of life, confidence and health.

Stress incontinence

Leakage when coughing, sneezing, laughing or during physical activity.

Urgency incontinence

A sudden, strong need to urinate with leakage before reaching the toilet.

Mixed incontinence

A combination of stress and urgency symptoms.
Important

The information on this site is educational and does not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Seek urgent medical care if you notice blood in your urine, have a high temperature, severe pain, cannot pass urine, or develop sudden weakness.

What you can do now

Why seeking help matters

Early assessment and appropriate care can reduce symptoms, improve quality of life and restore confidence.

Accurate assessment

An examination helps identify the type of symptoms and rule out other conditions.

Fewer symptoms

The right treatment reduces leakage and discomfort.

Confidence

Pelvic floor training and a clear plan bring back everyday comfort.

Online test
