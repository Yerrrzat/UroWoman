For patients

Types of symptoms

The type of symptoms helps the doctor choose tests and a treatment approach. One woman may have signs of more than one type.

Stress

Leakage happens during effort: coughing, sneezing, laughing, running, jumping or lifting. There may be no urge to urinate beforehand.

Urgency

A sudden, strong urge that is hard to hold back. Leakage may happen on the way to the toilet or at the sound of running water.

Mixed

Both leakage on effort and episodes after a strong urge. Care usually focuses on the symptoms that bother you most.

What to note before your appointment

  • when and in which situations the symptoms began;
  • how many times you urinate during the day and at night;
  • how much fluid you usually drink;
  • any pregnancies, births, operations, infections or new medicines.
Do not diagnose yourself. Infection, prolapse, side effects of medicines and other conditions can cause similar symptoms.
