Initial assessment pathway

This pathway helps you avoid missing important details at the first visit and identify patients who need specialist care in good time.

Symptoms, history and impact on quality of life
↓
ICIQ-SF (approved version) and a 3-day bladder diary
↓
Examination, pelvic floor assessment and cough test as indicated
↓
Urinalysis; post-void residual and other tests as clinically indicated
↓
Working classification of symptoms and a shared first-line plan

Reasons for referral

Haematuria, pain, recurrent infections, suspected prolapse or fistula, neurological symptoms, significant post-void residual, previous pelvic surgery and diagnostic uncertainty all require more detailed assessment.

Important

This pathway is an educational aid, not a clinical instruction in itself. The extent of assessment depends on the individual patient.
