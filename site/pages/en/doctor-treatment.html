Management and follow-up pathway

Management is built around the leading symptom type, the patient's goals, contraindications and the results of the initial assessment.

First line

  • patient education and agreeing an achievable goal;
  • pelvic floor muscle training for stress or mixed incontinence;
  • bladder training for urgency symptoms;
  • addressing modifiable factors: constipation, smoking, caffeine, body weight, fluid intake.

Review and escalation

At follow-up, reassess symptom severity, adherence, side effects and quality of life. If the response is inadequate, discuss drug treatment, further investigations or referral to a urogynaecologist in line with local protocols.

Communication principle

Decisions about medicines and surgery are made after discussing expected benefits, limitations, risks, alternatives and the patient's reproductive plans.

Important

This page does not contain prescribing regimens. Medicines and procedures are prescribed by a qualified doctor after a face-to-face assessment.
