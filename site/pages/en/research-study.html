Research

Research: transparent data and methods

The research section brings together verifiable national and international data on symptom prevalence, risk factors and women's quality of life.

What every result must include

  • the research question and design;
  • a description of the sample, period and inclusion criteria;
  • data collection and analysis methods;
  • limitations and a link to the primary source.

Protecting participants

The platform publishes only aggregated or anonymised data. Any research involving personal data requires a legal basis, participants' consent and approval from a local ethics committee.

Editorial standard: figures and clinical conclusions are published only after source checking and expert review.
