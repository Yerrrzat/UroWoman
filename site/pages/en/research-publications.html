Risk factors and quality of life in women with urinary incontinence in Kazakhstan

Ryspayeva Zh., Khismetova Z., Serikova-Esengeldina D., et al. Risk Factors and Quality of Life in Women with Urinary Incontinence in Kazakhstan: A Multicenter Case–Control Study. Int J Environ Res Public Health. 2026;23(7):893. doi:10.3390/ijerph23070893

An age-matched multicentre case–control study conducted from February to May 2025 in healthcare facilities across Kazakhstan. Quality of life was measured with the Kazakh and Russian versions of the I-QOL questionnaire.

2,061women in the study
687participants with urinary incontinence
51.8%of them had stress incontinence
27.8% / 20.4%urgency / mixed

Key findings

  • Urinary incontinence was independently associated with vaginal delivery (OR 1.48), multiple birth (OR 2.26), macrosomia (OR 1.83) and BMI ≥ 25 kg/m² (OR 2.08).
  • Women with urgency incontinence had the greatest impairment in quality of life.
  • The authors recommend prevention through weight management, antenatal counselling, postpartum follow-up and early pelvic floor assessment.

How to read publications

Check the authors, journal, date, sample, study type and conflicts of interest. A case–control design shows associations but does not prove causation or measure prevalence in the population. A single publication does not replace a clinical guideline.
